## Real-World Test
* **Test Machine:** 3-month-old Windows laptop (RTX 4050, clean install, no heavy bloatware).
* **Disabled:** Auto-start entries in **Logon**, **Scheduled Tasks**, and **Services** for Google, Zoom, Discord, Steam, Epic Games, Google Play, and ASUS Armoury Crate (replaced by G-Helper).
* **Kept:** NVIDIA services (required for GPU performance and 240 FPS frame locking).
* **Result:** Boot time dropped from **7.8s to 7.4s**. On a newer, clean machine, the main benefit is eliminating silent background updaters and freeing up RAM; older or more cluttered systems see much larger boot-time gains.
